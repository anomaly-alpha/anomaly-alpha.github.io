INVINCIBLE GTG WISH EVENT

Limited-Time Summon Event — Chips, Shop Levels & Trophy Road

by Anomaly · Updated Nov 7, 2026

Also known as

Ubisoft does not ship this event under the name "Wish Event" in game — that name is community coinage. Players and creators use all of the following for the same event, and they are interchangeable rather than different events:

  • Jackpot event
  • Wish token event
  • Jackpot wish event
  • Wish event

This page keeps calling it the Wish Event for consistency, but if a creator says "being that it is a jackpot wish type event" or titles a video "jackpot event", it is this event: the same shop, the same trophy road, the same pass, under a different wrapper.

Does the Wish Event pay gems?

No. The Wish Event is a hero and gear acquisition event. Its shop pays out heroes, hero dossiers, equipment tickets, artifact XP, and artifacts — not gems. It does not add to the weekly gem total on the gem calculator, and it does not change the 500 gems per cycle that The Long Haul and Earth's Defenders pay.

That matters for planning: if you are chasing gem income, the Wish Event is a spend, not an income source. If you are chasing specific heroes or artifact resources, it is one of the most efficient events in the game — which is a different question with a different answer.

What the Wish Event actually is

The Wish Event is a recurring limited-time event built around summoning from a dedicated event shop. The community calls it the Wish Event, Wish Token Event, or Jackpot Event, and Ubisoft wraps each run in a different theme — egg chips at Easter, hats around July 4th, Liberty chips for the America run, pumpkin tokens at Halloween, cake chips for the anniversary. The wrapping changes; the loop underneath does not.

The loop is: play the event's GDA operation and complete missions to earn event currency → spend that currency summoning in the event shop → every summon raises the shop's level → higher shop levels improve your odds of Elite heroes and of the run's special chip → the special chip is exchanged for the run's headline rewards. Layered on top since February 2026 is Trophy Road, a milestone track that runs alongside the shop.

The two event currencies

Ubisoft documents two event-only currencies: GDA Event Chips and GDA Special Chips. Both are obtainable only during events, and both can only be spent in the event shop. Community guides usually call them blue chips and, depending on the run's theme, egg chips, hats, Liberty chips, pumpkin tokens, or cake chips.

Shop levels 1–10, and why level 10 matters

The event shop has ten levels. There is a progress bar above the shop, and it fills as you summon — so the level is a direct measure of how much you have spent, not a separate upgrade you buy. Each level shifts the odds:

  • Elite hero odds rise with the shop level, and higher levels spread that chance across the full hero pool available in the shop.
  • Special chip odds are close to nothing early. Community breakdowns from recent runs put the special chip at roughly 1% around level 9 and about 2% cumulative at level 10, which is why nearly every guide says to aim for level 10 or not bother.
  • Below level 7–8 most players report seeing no special chips at all. That is the level band where the event either becomes a jackpot or a sump.

The practical consequence: a half-invested Wish Event is the worst outcome. Either push to the top levels, or treat the event as a cheap source of Elite heroes and dossiers from the low-level shop and stop.

Shop-level odds above are community-reported observations from recent runs rather than published rates, and they can change between runs.

Trophy Road

Trophy Road arrived in game update 2.14 on 10 February 2026 as an official feature: a progression track that fills as you earn Trophy Road Tokens during limited-time events, unlocking rewards as you pass milestones. In practice it is the event's second reward lane, running parallel to the shop.

Tokens come from ordinary event actions. Across recent runs the reported sources were:

  • Buying items from the event shop
  • Reaching new event shop levels
  • Refreshing the event shop
  • Logging in on separate days of the event
  • Completing event missions and the event operation

Milestone rewards have included event currency, GDA Ops access cards, and on some runs a free special chip at a fixed milestone. If a run offers a free special chip on the Trophy Road, take it before you spend down the shop — that chip feeds back into more shop investment.

The event operation and the hourly refresh

Each Wish Event ships with a dedicated GDA operation. Running it is the main repeatable source of GDA Event Chips, so it is where most of your summon currency comes from if you are not buying bundles. Some runs restrict the roster — for example limiting the operation to Earth's Defenders heroes — so read the operation's own requirements before you assume your best team is eligible.

The event shop also refreshes on an hourly timer for free. This is the single biggest lever for a low-spend run: the free refreshes are limited by the clock, not by currency, but the quality of what they offer is governed by your shop level. Burning refreshes at level 3 wastes them. Hold them until the shop is at its final level, ideally level 9 or 10, and spend the last day or two picking through refreshes instead.

Is the Wish Event free to play friendly?

Broadly, no — and it is worth being blunt about that, because the event is designed around investment. The community consensus across multiple runs is:

  • The paid GDA Pass adds guaranteed special chips, and skipping it noticeably caps what the event returns.
  • Creators describe going "all in" as spending gems in the tens of thousands before the shop's top levels pay off.
  • Players who cannot commit either are usually better off sitting the run out and saving for an event whose returns do not depend on depth.

If you want to participate cheaply anyway, the shape of the cheap run is consistent across guides: buy out the shop up to around level 5 to bank Elite heroes and dossiers, skip the deep investment, and do not spend your hourly refreshes until the shop has climbed as far as it is going to go.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Wish Event give gems?

No. The Wish Event pays heroes, dossiers, equipment tickets, artifact XP, and gear from its event shop. Gem income still comes from The Long Haul and Earth's Defenders, login rewards, PvP payouts, and promo codes.

What is the highest event shop level?

The event shop runs from level 1 to level 10. Every summon raises the shop level bar, and higher levels carry better odds of Elite heroes and of the event's special chip. Level 10 is the level most guides aim for, because the special chip odds only become meaningful in the last two levels.

Is the Wish Event free to play friendly?

Broadly no. Community consensus is that the Wish Event rewards go up steeply with investment, and that getting real value usually means buying the GDA Pass, spending gems in the tens of thousands, or both. A low-spend approach is to push the shop toward level 5 and then rely on the free hourly shop refreshes.

How long does the Wish Event run?

Recent runs have lasted about five to six days. Because the schedule rotates and varies, this page does not publish fixed dates, so check the in-game event tab for the current window.
