How many gems can I earn per week?

Players can earn approximately 4,043 gems per week from all sources: events (500), PvP (~1,850 with default Elite II rank 13 settings), login rewards (1,393), and promo codes (300). Your actual total varies based on PvP league and rank selections.

What is the best way to earn free gems?

Login rewards are the most reliable source at 1,393 gems/week guaranteed. PvP offers the highest potential but varies by league and rank. Events require competitive rankings within the top 5-10%. Promo codes are the easiest — just tap to reveal and redeem for instant gems or hero rewards.

How do PvP league multipliers work?

Each league has its own payout brackets. Higher leagues pay more gems and PvP Currency at every rank. For example, Elite II at rank 13 pays 520 gems in Restricted Arena, while the same rank in Intern pays only 80 gems. PvP Currency rewards follow the same pattern.

How much do login rewards give?

Login rewards total 1,393 gems per week: daily login gives 130/day (910/week), weekly gives 460 (60+400), and monthly gives 90/4 = 23/week. Combined monthly total is approximately 5,572 gems.

Are there any new promo codes?

There are 37 active promo codes with rewards including gems, hero shards, and Hero Shop Tickets. Generate a verification code in your game settings (top left LVL), then redeem at redeem.invincible.ubisoft.barcelona. Relaunch the game and rewards appear in a pop up. New codes are released through official game channels and community events.

What events give gem rewards?

Two recurring events offer gem rewards: The Long Haul (300 gems, top 5%) and Earth's Defenders (200 gems, top 10%). Both require active participation and competitive performance. Alliance membership significantly improves your chances of reaching the top thresholds.

What is the demotion zone in PvP?

If your rank reaches 86 or higher during Alliance War, you enter the demotion zone. After the war concludes, players at or above rank 86 risk being demoted to a lower league, which reduces their gem income tier in subsequent seasons.

Where do I enter promo codes?

Open your game and go to account settings (top left LVL). Under Verification Code, press generate and copy the code. Go to redeem.invincible.ubisoft.barcelona and enter your code. Relaunch the game and rewards appear in a pop up.

Are event rewards the same every cycle?

Yes. The Long Haul (300 gems, top 5%) and Earth's Defenders (200 gems, top 10%) repeat each cycle with the same reward structure. The strategies and payout thresholds remain consistent, so you can plan your approach each time.

Can I earn gems without being in an alliance?

Yes, but your income will be lower. Login rewards (1,393/week) and promo codes work without an alliance. Events and Alliance War PvP require alliance membership for score multipliers. Restricted and Open Arena are available to all players regardless of alliance status.

How often do new promo codes release?

New codes release regularly through official game channels, typically during events, holidays, content creator partnerships, and community milestones. No expiry date or schedule is published for any code: Ubisoft's support article says expiry depends on the drop, either an activity timer tied to an event or a redemption cap on how many players can claim it.

Where can I find new promo codes?

Follow the official Invincible Guarding the Globe social accounts on Instagram, Twitter/X, and Facebook, join community Discord servers, watch content creator streams, and check in-game announcement banners. Some codes have also appeared as QR codes during live streams or events. This guide is updated as new codes are discovered — bookmark the code page for the latest list.

What should I spend Hero Shop Tickets on?

Hero Shop Tickets can be exchanged for hero shards and upgrade materials. Prioritize heroes that fit your core team composition rather than spending on random shards — focused upgrades provide better long-term value for your roster.

How much do I need to play to earn 4,043 gems per week?

Log in daily for 1,393 guaranteed gems (about 5 minutes). Complete daily operations for the full login rewards. Play PvP arena battles each cycle. Events need more active time during their duration. Codes take seconds. Expect roughly 2-4 hours per week for full income.

What's the best league to stay in for gem income?

Higher leagues pay more in every mode. Elite II at rank 13 earns 520 gems per arena in Restricted and Open, while Invincible league at the same rank earns significantly more. Climb as high as you can maintain without risking demotion at rank 86.

Can I use the same promo code on multiple accounts?

Promo codes in Invincible Guarding the Globe are typically single-use per account. Each code can only be redeemed once per game account. Sharing codes with alliance members is fine, but each person needs their own redemption.
