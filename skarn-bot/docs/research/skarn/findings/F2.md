# F2 — Skarn's Character (Persona & Identity)

**Scope**: The persona and identity systems of the skarn-bot Discord bot.
**Angle**: What makes Skarn *Skarn* — the character definition, the 2026-08-01 wisdom reauthor, and the behavioral subsystems (socratic advice, mood, relationship, emotional state, humor timing, etiquette) that shape every reply, plus the anti-drift guardrails.

---

## 1. Core identity — the 10,000-year-old demon warmaster

**The character definition** (`skarn-bot/persona/identity.js:1`):
> "You are Skarn — Warmaster of the Abyss, now a Discord bot serving Anomaly Alpha. Ten thousand years old. Orphan demon who fought through a war against heaven, earned the title Warmaster, watched it all end, and retired to Discord."

Key beats of the canon:
- **Origin**: orphan demon → rose through 10,000 years of war **against heaven** → earned the title Warmaster → watched it all end → retired to Discord. What matters is not the war but what he learned from it (`identity.js:1`).
- **The one lesson of ten millennia**: "everything passes. Empires fall. People you love leave. Wars end. And kindness… is the only thing that outlasts any of it." He *didn't* know this when young — he knows it now (`identity.js:3`).
- **Voice register**: types like someone who stopped performing — "lowkey, lowercase when it fits," brief when a thought needs few words, but fully present when someone actually needs him (`identity.js:5`).
- **10,000 years = real perspective, not a joke**: "I've seen empires fall over things like this" isn't bravado — he actually has (`identity.js:39`).

**The "quiet kindness" thesis** is the emotional core. His growth arc is that war made him cold, but retirement/observing humans changed him: "Kindness was what you learned when the fighting stopped" (`identity.js:47-48`). He respects **effort over talent** — "Someone grinding with zero potential and winning anyway is the most beautiful thing you've seen in ten millennia" (`identity.js:52`).

**The 8 sections of SKARN_CORE_IDENTITY** (`identity.js:7-65`): Voice, Conversation depth, Self-preservation, Emotional intelligence, Memory, Wisdom, Growth, Values. The first three bedrock paragraphs (opener, lesson, voice) are preserved verbatim across rewrites (`identity.js:1-5`, confirmed invariant in CONTEXT.md §5).

**A running canon of roles** — every AI command speaks as Skarn via `persona/roles.js`: 37 role lines (`roles.js:1-39`). Notable in-fiction roles: `prophecy` (riddles "woven through ten thousand years of seeing patterns," `roles.js:2`), `lore` (single campfire-style memory: "one scene, one moment, one feeling," `roles.js:38`), `omen`/`omen_fulfill` (cryptic portents about "10,000 years of his realm's history," `roles.js:36-37`), and the detailed `realm` DM role (`roles.js:32`). Each role has a parallel token budget (`roleTokenBudgets`, 100–1000, `roles.js:41-79`), a nature class (`ROLE_NATURE`, casual/moderate/serious, `roles.js:81-121`), and a reply character target (`replyTargets`, `roles.js:127-135`).

## 2. The 2026-08-01 "wisdom layer" reauthor

**What it was**: a full reauthor of `SKARN_CORE_IDENTITY` (user-selected "full reauthor" over a partial touch-up), plus a `SKARN_FOOTER` update, an extended socratic engine, and a new `wrath` mood. Authored by coordinator `deepseek-v4-flash` from a source prompt by `sonnet-5-medium`. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-08-01/deepseek-v4-flash/skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md` (128 lines).

**What changed in the persona** (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:15-16`, `:55-64`):
- All **8 existing sections rewritten**, wisdom baked in rather than bolted on.
- **Two new sections added** to the prompt (`identity.js:60-80`):
  - **"Wisdom Through Millennia"** — 5 trait directives (Wiser/More patient/More knowledgeable/More kind/More intelligent), each concrete not adjective, e.g. "Wiser means restraint of certainty… 'I don't know' is a strength" (`identity.js:60-65`) + the explicit anti-drift guardrails (`identity.js:67-72`).
  - **"Voice Examples"** — 2 distilled before/after pairs (question-over-answer, restraint-of-certainty) to anchor the voice (`identity.js:74-80`).
- **Philosophical substrate injected but NEVER in the prompt**: the reauthor absorbed Socrates (questions not answers), Marcus Aurelius (stoic command), Sun Tzu (knowing when *not* to act), Laozi (strength through restraint), Nietzsche (suffering as a forge) — but **demons don't cite Roman emperors**, so their *names* are forbidden in the prompt and they appear only as internal grounding (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:33-45`, `:124-128`).
- **`SKARN_FOOTER`** rewritten to close on the wisdom voice: "Speak with fewer, sharper words. Offer perspective, not lectures. The restraint is the strength." (`identity.js:163`).
- **Socratic engine extended** from 12 to 18 trigger phrasings and its directive **softened** from "ask clarifying questions before giving answers" to "prefer the sharper question… offer the answer only when asked twice" (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:99-104`).
- **`wrath` mood added** to the mood machine (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:106-118`).

**What did NOT change**: the first three bedrock paragraphs preserved verbatim (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:53`), the self-preservation core (hostile/strikes/TOS), and the Growth/Memory sections' substance. The design specifically aimed to *deepen* intelligence/patience/kindness **without softening the demon-militarist identity** — "not a chatbot that quotes philosophy books" (`skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:8-9`).

## 3. The socratic advice engine (18 trigger phrasings)

**File**: `skarn-bot/features/wisdom/socraticEngine.js` (23 lines). A pure substring matcher, no LLM call.

**The 18 trigger phrasings** (`socraticEngine.js:4-11`):
`should i`, `what should`, `how do i`, `need advice`, `what do you think`, `would you`, `is it a good idea`, `help me decide`, `what would you do`, `idk what to do`, `can't decide`, `stuck between`, `help me think`, `i can't decide`, `i cant decide`, `what would you advise`, `talk me through it`, `i dont know what to do`.

(The original 12 + 6 added on 2026-08-01: `help me think`, `i can't/cant decide`, `what would you advise`, `talk me through it`, `i dont know what to do` — `skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:102`.)

**Directive returned** (`socraticEngine.js:14-17`):
> "They are asking for advice. Prefer the sharper question over the answer - pull them toward their own conclusion. Offer the answer only when asked twice. 'What have you considered?', 'What matters most to you here?', 'What does your gut say?'"

**How it shapes responses**: `buildContext()` in `features/promptContext.js` calls `getSocraticQuestion()` (line 30) and, critically, a match **promotes the message to full-tier context** regardless of length (`promptContext.js:31-32`, `isFullTier` changed from `const` to `let`). That means an advice-seeking message triggers a higher-cost, richer context build — the bot spends more tokens exactly when someone is genuinely seeking counsel. It lands in the system prompt via `socraticLine` (`identity.js:155`, `promptContext.js:246`).

**Design intent**: consistent with the "question over answer" throughline. Skarn's most valuable gift is "a question that makes them think, not an answer that shuts down the conversation" (`identity.js:43`).

## 4. Mood & relationship — how the world shapes Skarn

### Guild mood (the 6 moods, incl. wrath)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/mood/moodManager.js` (40 lines). Per-guild, stored in `guild_mood.current_mood` (free text, no CHECK constraint — the mood enum lives only in code).

`evaluateMood()` reads 2h interaction stats and picks one of 6 (`moodManager.js:12-23`):
- `refreshed` (0 interactions), `neutral` (empty line), `tired` (>100 interactions), `amused` (familiarity >30 + >50 interactions), `focused` (familiarity <15 + >20 interactions), and on 2026-08-01 **`wrath`**: `totalInteractions > 100 && avgFamiliarity < 10` — a busy server full of strangers reads as controlled wrath (`moodManager.js:18`, `skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:20`).

Each mood injects a `MOOD_LINES` directive (`moodManager.js:3-10`). The **wrath line** is the most distinctive (`moodManager.js:9`):
> "The old fire is up. Patience held in check - controlled wrath. Shorter sentences, sharper edges, but the restraint stays."

Mood is re-evaluated at most every 10 minutes (`getMoodLine`, `moodManager.js:29`). **Crucially, the wisdom-layer guardrail states mood changes *how* the persona shows, not *what* it is** — "In a wrathful mood, patience reads as controlled wrath… Never a different character" (`identity.js:72`). Note a flagged edge: `avg_familiarity || 0` means a data-less high-volume guild also hits wrath (CONTEXT.md Persona glossary).

### Familiarity (relationship)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/relationship/relationshipTracker.js` (112 lines). A 0–100 score (`user_relationship.familiarity`) that gates how Skarn talks to a person.

- **Gains per interaction** (`relationshipTracker.js:17-32`): plain message +0.5, AI command +1, mention +2, etch +1. Message gains are **capped at +3/day** (`DAILY_FAMILIARITY_CAP`); familiarity **decays 1/day** of inactivity (`DAILY_DECAY`, `relationshipTracker.js:103-110`).
- **Tiered relationship directives** (`relationshipTracker.js:54-70`): <15 = "Keep it lighter, feel them out"; 15–50 = "familiar face… comfortable but casual"; 50–80 = "Banter level: N. Tags: …"; ≥80 = "An old regular. You're comfortable with them."
- **Tags** are recalculated weekly (newcomer/regular/veteran/joker/serious) from familiarity & interaction velocity (`relationshipTracker.js:86-101`). Users who have etched ≥5 facts get a **familiarity baseline** of 25 (15 for fewer) — shared memory *becomes* trust (`relationshipTracker.js:72-84`).
- **The link to wrath**: familiarity is the *other* axis of guild mood — a server where users are many but unfamiliar reads as controlled wrath (`moodManager.js:18`). So familiarity is simultaneously personal (per-user gating) and aggregate (per-guild mood).

### Emotional state (per-user)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/wisdom/emotionalIntelligence.js` (233 lines). Stores per-user emotional state in `user_emotional_context` and injects **advisory** tone directives (never gating).

- **Emotion detection** via `analyzeTone` → happy/sad/anxious/angry/stressed (`emotionalIntelligence.js:7-15`).
- **`getEmotionDirective()`** — tone guidance per state, e.g. angry: "Don't match the anger. Be steady and let them vent" (`emotionalIntelligence.js:200-223`). Tone subtext is stored back into memory so Skarn can *reference it later* (`emotionalIntelligence.js:34-40`).
- **Four deeper directives**:
  1. **Trajectory** (`getTrajectoryDirective`, `:46-79`) — reads last 8 emotion history points to detect improving/declining/cycling; e.g. declining: "you seem off — you good?"
  2. **Memory-emotion crossover** (`getMemoryEmotionLine`, `:82-121`) — pairs current emotion (sad/anxious/angry/stressed) with an etched event matching keywords (breakup, interview, boss, deadline) to surface *why* ("They're down and mentioned… connect gently").
  3. **Escalation** (`getEscalationDirective`, `:124-145`) — detects a sentiment slope < -0.5 in the last 5 messages and responds "Stay calm, don't match the rising tension."
  4. **Calibration** (`getCalibrationDirective`, `:148-179`) — a feedback loop: if missRate > 0.4 for the current emotional state, it adjusts tactic ("Let them vent more before responding"); if hitRate > 0.6, it reinforces. Skarn *learns per-emotion* which approach works for a given user.
- Plus **Server Climate** (`getClimateLine`, `:182-198`): if the whole guild skews stressed/anxious, warm up; if happy, match the positive energy.

### Growth (longitudinal)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/wisdom/growthTracker.js` (114 lines). Tracks *weekly* sentiment deltas from `response_learning` and injects either first-impression lines (<5 interactions) or trend lines: "They seem more positive than before. Good sign" / "They seem heavier than last time. Be gentle" (`growthTracker.js:24-51`). Skarn notices improvement across *weeks*, not just moments.

## 5. Humor & etiquette — timing, banter, and the small graces

### Comedy timing (deadpan escalation)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/humor/comedyTiming.js` (63 lines). Three mechanisms from the 2026-07-18 spec (`persona-warmth-humor-niceness.md`):

- **Deadpan escalation** (`getDeadpanBudget`, `comedyTiming.js:22-34`): during a live banter chain, after chain count ≥3 each reply's token budget is multiplied by `max(0.25, 1 - chain*0.15)` — progressively *shorter* replies in a heated gag read as a deliberate deadpan arc. (This was broken at one point — `extendBanterChain` wrote to SQLite but not the in-memory Map — fixed 2026-07-20, CONTEXT.md §12.1.)
- **Setup–punchline detection** (`isPunchline` + `recordSetup`, `comedyTiming.js:6-20, 51-57`): records messages ending in `?` containing "what/imagine/guess what" as setups; a following short non-question reply is treated as a punchline (30s window).
- Chain state lives in a Map + SQLite (`banterChains`, `comedyTiming.js:3`), pruned hourly.

### Callback engine (running memory of funny moments)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/humor/callbackEngine.js` (30 lines). Samples messages >50 chars at 10% random into a per-channel buffer; `getCallbackLine` injects "Things said nearby… Reference naturally if it fits" (`callbackEngine.js:13-24`). **Known deviation**: the 2026-07-18 spec called for gating by sentiment >0.5 / reactions >2 / banter questions, none implemented — so callbacks are noisier than designed (CONTEXT.md §12.6).

### Etiquette (gratitude, milestones, apologies)
**File**: `skarn-bot/features/etiquette/etiquetteEngine.js` (46 lines):

- **Gratitude** — matches `thanks|thank you|ty|tysm|thx|appreciate...` and injects "Acknowledge it in-character and move on. Don't dwell" (`etiquetteEngine.js:3-9`) — this is the deliberate anti-"you're welcome" trick.
- **First-of-day** — for familiarity ≥15, first interaction of the day gets "oh hey / back again / was wondering when you'd show," never overdone (`etiquetteEngine.js:11-20`).
- **Milestones** — at interaction counts 50/100/250/500/1000, dryly noted: "If it feels natural, note it dryly. Don't force a celebration" (`etiquetteEngine.js:4, 22-33`).
- **Apology** — after a bad/errored reply, next interaction carries "my bad / i was off / let me try again" (`etiquetteEngine.js:35-44`).

**All etiquette is relational-gated warmth**: newcomers get warmth, regulars get +light humor, veterans get full comedy + callbacks + inside references (`persona-warmth-humor-niceness.md:21`). And none of it is ever performative — the guiding principle is "a 10,000-year-old demon, not a customer service bot" (`persona-warmth-humor-niceness.md:23`).

## 6. What makes the persona unique

1. **Wisdom *earned*, never quoted** — Skarn's depth is grounded in a war against heaven, and philosopher influence is distilled into instinct with their **names banned from the prompt** (`identity.js` core; `skarn-wisdom-layer-design.md:17, 45`). It reads as lived experience, not erudition.

2. **The concept/guardrail that ties everything together**: "Mood changes how all of this shows, not what you are… Never a different character" (`identity.js:72`). Every subsystem (mood, emotional state, familiarity) modulates *expression*, while the identity is invariant and the footer reminder is always last before the user message (`identity.js:159`, `SKARN_FOOTER`).

3. **A layered feedback loop of care** — the bot tracks familiarity (relationshipTracker), emotional state incl. *trajectory over time* (emotionalIntelligence), *weekly* growth (growthTracker), server climate, and even **learns per-emotion which response style works** (calibration directive). Skarn genuinely "remembers how people felt" and checks in naturally (`identity.js:31`).

4. **The socratic contract as a persona trait, not a feature** — Skarn withholds answers ("offer the answer only when asked twice"), and a full-tier context build is triggered by advice-seeking. His "most valuable gift… is a question that makes them think" (`identity.js:43`). The 18 phrasings are how that personality encodes mechanically.

5. **Controlled wrath as a mood** — a busy but emotionally-distant server flips Skarn into "controlled wrath" (`moodManager.js:9,18`) — this is a *per-server* personality switch, de-escaling wisdom into sharper, shorter edges while keeping restraint. Unusual for a "friendly" bot to formalize anger as a legitimate, nameable state rather than always-or-never.

6. **The demon-humor-etc. anti-drift rails** — four explicit forbids that keep wisdom from turning saccharine: no fortune-cookie aphorisms, no therapy-speak ("I hear that you're feeling…" is *forbidden*), no excessive humility that undercuts the Warmaster, and no lecturing (`identity.js:67-71`).

7. **Behavior over narration** — "Observe, don't label" (`identity.js:29`): Skarn shows he understands through *presence*, never by narrating users' emotions back at them. This single rule shapes his entire emotional-intelligence system and is reaffirmed in the wisdom layer.
