Privacy Policy

Last updated August 22, 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller for this Site is Anomaly (community developer). The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. For questions about data practices, contact us via GitHub Issues.

2. Information We Collect

This Site uses Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to understand pseudonymous or aggregated usage such as page views, referral sources, general geographic region, device type, and browser type. Google Analytics may process an IP address to derive approximate location; the tag is configured with IP anonymization. We do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, account credentials, or other directly identifying information. The Site has no user registration, no forms, and no server-side application data storage. Calculations are performed locally in your browser. Your PvP league and rank selections are saved only in your browser's localStorage and are not sent to us.

3. Third-Party Processors

Google LLC (Google Analytics). Google Analytics is used to measure pseudonymous site usage. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. GitHub, Inc. hosts the Site via GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy policy is available at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies. GitHub Issues — any contact or correspondence submitted via GitHub Issues is publicly visible by design. Do not include sensitive personal information in GitHub Issues.

4. Cookies

Google Analytics uses persistent analytics cookies, including _ga and _ga_* cookies, to distinguish visitors, maintain analytics state, and measure site usage across visits. These are not session-only cookies; their retention is controlled by Google's Analytics configuration and policies. These cookies are set by Google Analytics when the tag runs. GitHub Pages may also set strictly necessary technical cookies required for content delivery network functionality, as described in GitHub's privacy policy.

This Site is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

To exercise your CCPA rights — including the right to know, delete, and opt out — contact us via GitHub Issues. Do Not Track: The current Google Analytics tag on this Site does not use browser Do Not Track (DNT) signals to disable analytics collection.
