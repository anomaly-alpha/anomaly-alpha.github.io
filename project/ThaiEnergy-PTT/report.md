## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

PTT Group is Thailand's largest and most strategically important energy conglomerate, operating across the entire oil and gas value chain—from upstream exploration through midstream gas transmission to downstream refining, petrochemicals, retail, and power generation. As of 2025, PTT Group had a combined market capitalization of approximately THB 1.92 trillion (USD 58 billion), with the Thai Ministry of Finance holding a 51% controlling stake.

**Current state as of mid-2026**:

- **Consolidated 1Q2026 revenue**: THB 718.7 billion (+13% QoQ, +3% YoY); EBITDA: THB 115.9 billion (+49% QoQ, +23% YoY); net income: THB 25.7 billion (+10% YoY)
- **Net Debt/EBITDA**: 1.34x — comfortable leverage with investment-grade ratings (Moody's Baa1, S&P BBB+, Fitch BBB+)
- **Committed CAPEX 2026–2030**: ~THB 1,015 billion (USD 31 billion)
- **Key strategic targets**: LNG volume of 10 MTPA by 2030 and 15 MTPA by 2035; Net Zero by 2050; oil & gas production reaching 620,000 boed by 2029

The group is navigating an extraordinarily turbulent geopolitical environment marked by the Middle East conflict, Strait of Hormuz closure, and resulting LNG supply disruptions, while simultaneously executing "The Great Rebalance" — a multi-pronged transformation strategy encompassing existing business optimization, new business expansion (EV, life sciences, hydrogen, CCS), and aggressive portfolio restructuring.
